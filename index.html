The details

Small things, done properly

Encrypted backups

Export everything to one file protected by a passphrase you choose. Restore it on any phone.

Fully offline

Every feature works with no connection, because nothing ever needs a server.

Matches your phone

Follow your wallpaper colours on Android 12 and newer, or keep the SafeKeep green.

Home-screen widgets

A quick note square, plus a note widget you can tick off without opening the app. Note text shows only while no PIN is set.

Share when you choose

Send a single note as plain text to any app. Nothing is shared unless you do it.

A safety net

Deleted notes wait in the trash for seven days, and archiving or deleting can be undone.

Only SafeKeep holds the key

Encrypted database

Every note lives in an AES-256 encrypted database. There is no plain-text copy.

Keys stay in the phone

The key is kept in Android’s hardware-backed secure storage, and sealed by your PIN if you turn one on.

Guessing is slowed down

With a PIN on, five wrong tries start a wait that doubles each time, from 30 seconds up to an hour.

Nothing to breach

There is no server and no account, so there is no database of users to leak.
