FAQ

Questions, answered straight

If something here is unclear, the security page goes into more detail.

The basics

What is SafeKeep?

A notes app for Android with notes, checklists, labels, colours and reminders. The difference is where your notes live: only on your phone, encrypted, with no account and no cloud.

Does it really work offline?

Yes. There is no server behind the app, so every feature works without a connection. The released app does not even have permission to use the internet.

Does it sync between devices?

Not at the moment. Notes stay on one phone. You can move them to another phone with an encrypted backup file.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. SafeKeep is Android only for now.

Can I import my notes from another app?

Not yet. For now, notes are created in SafeKeep or restored from a SafeKeep backup.

Security

What happens if I forget my PIN?

The notes cannot be opened. There is no reset link and no recovery, by design. You can erase the app and restore from an encrypted backup, so make one once you have notes worth keeping.

Can I use SafeKeep without a PIN?

Yes. You can skip the PIN during setup or turn it off later in Settings. Your notes stay encrypted on storage, but anyone who can use your unlocked phone can then open the app.

Can the developer read my notes?

No. Notes never leave your phone, there is no server to send them to, and the keys exist only on your device.

Is SafeKeep open source?

Not at the moment. What you can check yourself today: the app has no internet permission, each release has a published checksum, and the security page lists exactly how notes are protected and where the limits are.

Has it been audited?

No independent audit has been done yet. SafeKeep is built from standard, well-studied pieces (SQLCipher, AES-256-GCM, Android Keystore), and we would rather say that plainly than imply more.

Installing and updating

Why is it not on the Play Store?

SafeKeep is shared directly as a signed APK from this site. You do not need a store account to get it, and nothing about your download is tied to an identity.

Android warns me about installing unknown apps. Is that normal?

Yes. Android shows that warning for every app installed outside the Play Store. Download SafeKeep only from this site, and compare the checksum on the download page if you want to be certain the file is genuine.

How do I update?

Download the newer APK from the download page and install it over the old one. Your notes stay in place. The app cannot update itself because it has no internet access.

How do I move my notes to a new phone?

In Settings, create an encrypted backup and choose a passphrase. Copy the file to the new phone, install SafeKeep there and choose Restore from backup.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 7.0 and newer. Matching your wallpaper colours needs Android 12 or newer.
