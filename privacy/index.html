Privacy

We collect nothing

That is the whole policy. The rest of this page spells out what it covers.

No account

There is nothing to sign up for, so there is no profile of you.

No analytics

The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising code.

No servers

Your notes are never uploaded. There is nowhere to upload them to.

The app

Everything you write in SafeKeep is stored in an encrypted database on your phone. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your notes, usage data or anything else to anyone, including its developer.

Your notes leave the app only when you choose to move them: when you share a note as text, copy it to the clipboard, or create a backup file. Backup files are encrypted with a passphrase that you choose and that is never stored.

This website

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party scripts or fonts. The only thing saved in your browser is your light or dark theme choice, and it stays there.

Like any website, the company that hosts these pages can see basic technical details of each request, such as an IP address, in its server logs. We do not use those logs to identify or follow anyone.

Reminders and permissions

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by Android itself. Notifications show only the note’s title, and are hidden on the lock screen. The full list of what the app can access is on the security page.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that changes this, such as optional sync, it will be off by default, explained on this page first, and never switched on without you choosing it.
